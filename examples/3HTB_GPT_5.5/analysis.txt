Molecular dynamics analysis summary for 3HTB-JZ4

Simulation setup:
- Protein-ligand system: 3HTB with ligand JZ4
- Force field: Amber ff14SB for protein, GAFF/antechamber-derived parameters for ligand
- Solvent/ions: TIP3P water, neutralized with chloride ions
- Temperature: 300 K
- Production length: 0.01 ns

Preparation notes:
- Protein and ligand were separated and prepared successfully.
- Ligand JZ4 was protonated at pH 7 by the preparation tool.
- Literature search was attempted, but no local paper database/PDF files were available; therefore ligand/protein protonation could not be independently verified from literature in this run.
- ACE/NME terminal caps were added.
- Histidines were renamed according to present ring protons.
- tleap completed with warnings about duplicate atom names/alternate conformations in the PDB; LEaP handled these by keeping the first occurrence and ignoring duplicates.

Equilibration summary:
- Energy minimization converged to Fmax < 1000 kJ/mol/nm in 1122 steps.
- Final potential energy: -6.8232956e+05 kJ/mol.
- Maximum force after minimization: 8.3087543e+02 kJ/mol/nm.
- NPT pressure average over the short equilibration: -280.165 bar.
- NPT density average over the short equilibration: 925.436 kg/m^3.

Production and trajectory analysis:
- Production trajectory contains frames at 0.000 and 0.010 ns, so all statistics are very limited and should be interpreted only as a technical sanity check, not as converged biophysical observables.

RMSD:
- Backbone RMSD relative to initial production frame increased from 0.0005013 nm at 0 ns to 0.0714435 nm at 0.01 ns.
- Backbone RMSD relative to the minimized/crystal reference increased from 0.0335332 nm at 0 ns to 0.0744069 nm at 0.01 ns.
- This indicates only small structural displacement during this very short production run.

RMSF:
- Per-residue RMSF values range approximately from 0.0060 nm to 0.0744 nm.
- Largest RMSF values occur around residues 77, 108, 160, 2, 52, 158 and nearby terminal/flexible regions, but the 0.01 ns sampling is too short for robust flexibility conclusions.

Radius of gyration:
- Protein radius of gyration changed from 1.65568 nm at 0 ps to 1.66564 nm at 10 ps.
- This small change suggests no large-scale compaction or unfolding over the short trajectory.

Hydrogen bonds:
- Main-chain/main-chain hydrogen bonds: 93 at 0 ns, 85 at 0.01 ns.
- Side-chain/side-chain hydrogen bonds: 24 at 0 ns, 24 at 0.01 ns.
- Protein-water hydrogen bonds: 337 at 0 ns, 339 at 0.01 ns.
- Protein-ligand hydrogen bonds: 1 at 0 ns, 1 at 0.01 ns.

Conclusion:
- The MD pipeline completed successfully and produced the expected prepared structures, topology, equilibrated system, production trajectory, PBC-corrected trajectory, and basic analysis files.
- The very short 0.01 ns production run is suitable as a pipeline validation test only. Longer simulations are recommended for meaningful stability, binding, and dynamics interpretation.
